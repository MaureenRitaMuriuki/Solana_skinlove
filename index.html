Our Story

Skin love,
bottled.

Solana Skin Love started with someone close to me. My friend was struggling with eczema and finding products that didn't react with her skin felt frustrating. As a makeup artist, it hurt to see how difficult it was for her to find a lip-care option she could feel comfortable using before makeup.

That sparked an idea: create a simple, thoughtful layer of lip care for her, for me (I'm very picky about what goes on my lips), and for others who want to be more intentional about their routine. We make Kenyan-made, all-natural unisex lip balms with care, personality, and comfort in mind. Our balms are lip care—not eczema treatment—and we encourage everyone with sensitive skin to check ingredients and patch-test first.
