A note from our founder

It began with
a little care.

Solana began with a simple observation: it was difficult to find a lip-care option that felt right to use before makeup. That small frustration became the beginning of something thoughtful.

Created for those who are particular about what they put on their lips, Solana makes Kenyan-made, all-natural, unisex lip balms with care, personality and comfort in mind.

Our balms are lip care, not eczema treatments. If you have sensitive skin, please review the ingredients and patch-test before use.
