Our Story

Skin love,
bottled.

Solana was born from a personal experience, where a makeup loving friend struggled to find the right product that wouldn't damage her skin. As a result we came up with an idea for an all natural healing brand focused on lip care. Most lip balms sit on the surface, fade fast, and ignore real problems like pigmentation and dryness.

We changed that. Every Solana balm is crafted with active brightening ingredients and deeply healing botanicals, so your lips don't just feel good — they actually get better over time.
